What am I looking at?

SPHEREx is a NASA telescope that photographs the whole sky in 102 infrared colours. Each square pixel is about 6 arcseconds across. Every frame here is one SPHEREx photo of the same patch, lined up north-up. Stars and galaxies stay put; asteroids and comets drift from frame to frame.

Every frame sees a slightly different colour (shown as λ, in microns), so some brightness changes are just colour. Compare mode pairs frames of similar colour for you.

Data: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (SPHEREx), IMCCE SkyBoT, ESA Gaia DR3. Built for NASA Space Apps.
